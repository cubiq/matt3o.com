**UPDATE: [now live on Drop](https://drop.com/buy/drop-matt3o-mt3-3277-custom-keycap-set)!**

When we designed the MT3 keycap profile we had very specific _vintage_ keyboards in mind. One of them was the [IBM 3277](https://deskthority.net/wiki/IBM_3277_typewriter_keyboard) (and the whole 32xx series). So before venturing any further in colorful and extravagant color schemes I had to honor the 3277 by dedicating an MT3 set to it.

Hence I'm proud to introduce to you simply **"3277"**, the next MT3 double-shot to be produced in the coming months.

![](/3277-finally-the-keyset-mt3-was-designed-for/images/base.png)

I understand that the base kit could be a little boring for some of you, so I added some very exuberant accent colors you can play with: magenta, turquoise, tangerine, salmon, acid green and red.

![](/3277-finally-the-keyset-mt3-was-designed-for/images/accent.png)

I'm waiting for the final renders, in the meantime here you'll find some 2d examples of what you can do with it. The colored keys will be sold in one "Accent" kit so you can mix and match your harlequin keyboard.
